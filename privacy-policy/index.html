Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call or visit, and how we use it.

Overview

This policy explains what information Dickinson Water Damage Pros collects when you use this website or call our line. This site does not contain forms, accounts or checkout pages. The main way to reach us is by phone.

Information we receive

When you call, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell the dispatcher, such as your address, a description of the damage and your name. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message. The website itself may log basic technical data such as browser type and pages viewed through our hosting provider.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call or contact us by email instead.

How we use information

We use call details to dispatch a crew, to explain scope and price, to follow up on a job and to improve how the line is answered. We use email to reply to your message. We do not use this information for anything unrelated to your request.

Sharing information

Information you give us is shared with the licensed and insured restoration professionals who respond to your job, and with service providers who help us run the phone line and website. We may disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic cookies or analytics to understand which pages are visited. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them does not stop the site or the phone line from working.

Retention and security

We keep call and job records for as long as needed to handle the job, resolve questions and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Contact us at contact@dickinsonwaterdamagepros.com or call (844) 950-6900.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the current one.
