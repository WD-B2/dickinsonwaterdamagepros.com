Toilet or drain backing up with dark water?

If a toilet, tub or floor drain is pushing up dark water, or the whole house smells like a sewer, you are dealing with more than a clog. Water that comes up from the sewer line is contaminated, and cleaning it up is a specialized task known as sewage backup cleanup or Category 3 water damage restoration. Keep people and pets away and call a professional.

Sewage backup cleanup in Dickinson, TX covers the removal, disinfection and drying that follow a backed-up sewer line. Restoration professionals sort water into three categories, and sewage belongs to the worst one, Category 3. That means anything porous it touched, such as carpet, pad and drywall, is generally removed instead of cleaned.

Why sewage is different

Sewage carries bacteria and other pathogens, and it can leave residue on every surface it reaches. Ordinary mopping spreads it. Crews wear gloves, boots, eye protection and respirators, bag contaminated materials and treat the area with antimicrobial products after tear-out. Drying follows once surfaces are clean.

When sewage backup is an emergency

It is urgent whenever sewage is inside the home, since it is a health hazard from the first hour. The risk grows if children, older adults or anyone with a health condition lives in the house.

Stay out and stay safe

Do not wade in sewage, do not use the affected plumbing and do not run a household vacuum on it. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and call your electric provider or 911 before calling the restoration line. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911. Report a main-line blockage to your city or district.

What to do while you wait

  1. Stop using toilets, sinks and washing machines so more does not enter the home.
  2. Keep people and pets out of the affected rooms.
  3. Photograph the backup from the doorway without entering.
  4. Call your water and sewer provider to check whether the problem is in the main line.
  5. Call our 24/7 line.

Whose pipe is it?

A backup can come from your own drain line or from the public main. If the blockage is in the main, your provider clears it. If it is in your own line, a plumber does. In Dickinson, Galveston County WCID No. 1 is listed on the City of Dickinson website as the water company, and it can tell you whether a problem is on its side of the connection.

Ask who serves your address if you are unsure.

What cleanup involves

Crews extract the contaminated water with equipment that keeps it away from clean areas. Porous materials are removed and bagged, hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. Contents like rugs and upholstery are usually discarded.

The flood damage cleanup page describes the same Category 3 approach for outside water. After cleanup, ask the crew for a record of what was removed, because your insurer will want it.

Rebuilding after sewage

Once the structure is dry and clean, repair begins. Flooring, drywall and trim are replaced. The drying readings confirm the building is ready for new materials. Permits for rebuilding depend on the scope, and the City of Dickinson Building Permits and Inspections office at 4403 Highway 3 can tell you what applies.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration classifies sewage as Category 3 water, and that classification is the reason porous materials are removed and surfaces disinfected.

Sewage backup questions

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Dickinson?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes sewage calls at any hour, and the dispatcher sends a crew by address and loss size. Keep people out of the area and call right away. Same-day response is the goal when crews are available.

Why is my toilet pushing up dark water?

The sewer line may be blocked. Stop using plumbing, keep people out and call your provider and our line.

Can sewage-soaked carpet be cleaned?

Usually not. Porous materials that contact sewage are removed.
