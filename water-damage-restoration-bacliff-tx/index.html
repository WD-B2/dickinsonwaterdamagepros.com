Bacliff is a census-designated place, not an incorporated city, and that fact shapes almost everything about a water damage job here. There is no city hall to call for permits and no city utility to call for a main break. Water and sewer come from a municipal utility district, and building rules fall under Galveston County. Knowing that before a loss saves time on the phone.

When water is spreading in Bacliff

Bayside storms are the big risk. Bacliff sits on the western shore of Galveston Bay, which means surge and rain can arrive together, as they did with Hurricane Ike in 2008. Galveston County ran a debris removal program for unincorporated areas, Bacliff among them, after that storm. If bay water or rain is entering a home, treat it as Category 3 and keep people out of it.

When a supply line or appliance fails inside the house, the steps differ. Close the valve, lift valuables and call.

Stop the flow

Find the valve nearest the leak or close the main, and note the time you did.

Check the panel

Cut power to wet circuits only if you can reach the panel standing on dry ground.

Raise valuables

Move papers, electronics and small furniture to higher shelves or another room.

Avoid bay water

Water that came in from the street or the bay is contaminated, so keep everyone out.

Call for extraction

Tell dispatch which rooms are wet and whether the water is still rising.

Who to call in an unincorporated area

Galveston County lists Bacliff MUD as the public water and sewer provider for Bacliff addresses, with CenterPoint Energy as the power provider. A water main break or a sewer line problem at the street is the district's to handle. Floodplain permits for unincorporated property run through Galveston County's engineering and floodplain office in Galveston, so ask the county before a major rebuild. Dickinson's permit office does not cover Bacliff addresses.

Drainage on the Bayshore

Bacliff, San Leon and Bayview together form the area known as the Bayshore. Galveston County has advertised drainage work in Bacliff, including a storm sewer project along Jackson Avenue between 9th Street and Galveston Bay. Better drainage can reduce street flooding, though interior leaks and storm entry still need restoration. The storm water damage cleanup page covers wind-driven rain.

Services Bacliff residents ask for

Storm and tidal flooding lead, followed by plumbing and appliance failures. The flood damage cleanup page covers contaminated water. The appliance leak page covers hoses and supply lines. For a larger loss that needs equipment for several days, see structural drying. Our Kemah page covers the next bayside town to the north.

History that still matters

Bacliff began as Clifton-by-the-Sea, a seaside resort, and the community still has the low, flat bay-edge setting that name suggests. The 2020 census counted 9,677 residents.

Know your shut-offs before a loss

Find the main valve at the house and the meter at the street, and learn which one you are allowed to touch. The valve at the house is yours. The shutoff at the meter belongs to the district, so call Bacliff MUD if a leak is on the street side and you cannot isolate it.

Write the district's contact details and your account number on a card near the breaker panel. In a storm, that card saves minutes.

Insurance and documentation

The Texas Department of Insurance says standard homeowner policies exclude floods and that you need a separate flood policy. If you have a federal flood policy, the claim and documentation window is 60 days. Photograph the waterline on walls and the condition of flooring before cleanup, and keep receipts for emergency work.

What to do before a crew arrives in Bacliff

  • Shut off the main valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Keep power off in wet rooms until it is checked.
  • Lift small valuables off wet floors.
  • Photograph every wet room before cleanup.

Bacliff water damage questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Bacliff?

Bacliff uses ZIP code 77518, and we cover properties along SH 146, along FM 646 and on the streets between them. If your address is near the Bayview or San Leon boundary, the dispatcher will confirm coverage on the call.

Is emergency water removal available in Bacliff?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open at all hours, including nights and weekends, and crews are dispatched by address and loss size. Same-day response is the goal when crews are available.

Who do I call about a water main break in Bacliff?

Bacliff MUD provides water and sewer service. Call the district for the main, and call us if water has entered the house.

Do I need a permit to rebuild after a flood in Bacliff?

Often, yes. Because Bacliff is unincorporated, ask Galveston County's floodplain office about permits before work starts.
