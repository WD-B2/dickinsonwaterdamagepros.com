Kitchen Water Damage in Dickinson, TX

A sink leak, a dishwasher failure or a broken supply line can soak cabinets and flooring fast. Call to talk through kitchen water damage in Dickinson, TX.

Call (844) 950-6900See all services

Kitchen water damage restoration in Dickinson, TX deals with a room where water, appliances and wood cabinets share a small space. Water from a sink supply line, a dishwasher or a refrigerator ice-maker line tends to run down into the cabinet base and spread under flooring, and the damage can sit unseen behind toe kicks.

The kitchen is also where many insurance claims start. Because plumbing, appliances and finished surfaces are concentrated there, even a small leak can mean cabinets, countertops, flooring and drywall are all affected.

Where kitchen leaks begin

  • Faucet and sink supply lines under the counter.
  • Dishwasher supply lines, drain hoses and door seals.
  • Refrigerator water lines.
  • Garbage disposal and drain connections.
  • Leaks from a bathroom or upstairs room above the ceiling.

Signs of a hidden kitchen leak

Warped cabinet bases, a musty smell under the sink, loose or lifting flooring near the dishwasher, discolored caulk at the countertop and a floor that feels soft near the sink are all red flags.

What to do first

  1. Close the shut-off under the sink or the main water valve.
  2. Empty the cabinet under the sink and photograph the leak.
  3. Mop up standing water and move items off wet flooring.
  4. Keep the damaged hose or fitting.
  5. Call us if water reached flooring, walls or neighboring rooms.

Cabinets, toe kicks and the floor under them

Wood cabinets made of particleboard swell and lose strength when wet. Solid wood and plywood hold up better. Crews remove toe kicks and check the cavity behind them with a moisture meter, since water collects there. Flooring under cabinets is a particular challenge because it cannot be lifted without removing the cabinets. Sometimes drying through small openings or injection systems works, and sometimes the base cabinets must be pulled.

Countertops are rarely harmed by water directly, but moisture beneath them can loosen laminate and swell the substrate. Drywall behind the sink is checked and dried or replaced. The wall and drywall page explains the decision.

Dishwasher and appliance losses

Dishwasher leaks deliver gray water that carries food and detergent, so the cleanup plan is stricter than for a clean supply line. The appliance leak water damage page covers appliance-specific cleanup.

Repair after drying

Once readings are normal, repair begins. Flooring is replaced or refinished, cabinets are repaired or swapped and drywall is patched and painted. Matching cabinets and countertops can be the longest part of the rebuild, so ask about lead times early.

Keep your photos and the failed part for the insurer. The City of Dickinson Building Permits and Inspections office at 4403 Highway 3 can say whether plumbing or structural repair needs a permit.

Cooking and living while the kitchen dries

Keep the refrigerator door closed and move food to a cooler if the unit has to be unplugged or moved. Do not run the dishwasher or the disposal until a plumber has cleared the lines. A temporary prep area in another room helps the household keep eating while crews work. Ask the crew which outlets are safe to use, since extension cords near wet floors are a hazard.

Plan for several days of equipment noise and limited counter space.

Counters and backsplashes need attention too. Water that ran behind the sink can loosen the sealant at the backsplash and wet the drywall behind the tile. If the countertop is laminate over particleboard, the edges near the sink swell first. Check these spots in daylight, and mention them when the crew arrives so they are included in the moisture map.

Kitchen drying follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, which is why water behind cabinets and under flooring is measured instead of judged by eye.

The Texas Department of Insurance says sudden and accidental water damage, such as a broken washer hose, is typically covered and gradual leaks are not. Ask your insurer.

Kitchen water damage questions

Can wet cabinets be saved?

Solid wood and plywood often can. Particleboard that has swollen usually cannot.

Do I have to remove the cabinets to dry the floor?

Not always. Crews try drying through small openings first and remove cabinets when moisture remains.

How long does a kitchen take to dry?

Often several days. Readings decide.

Is a dishwasher leak covered by insurance?

Often if it was sudden. Ask your insurer.
