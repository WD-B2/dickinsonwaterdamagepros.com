Emergency Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Dickinson, TX

A wet ceiling can collapse without warning. Keep people out from under it, and call our 24/7 line for ceiling water damage in Dickinson, TX.

Call (844) 950-6900See all services

Ceiling water damage repair in Dickinson, TX begins with a question: where is the water coming from? A brown stain on a ceiling is a symptom, and the source may be a roof leak, a pipe in the attic, an air conditioner drain pan or a bathroom above. Fixing the stain without finding the source means it comes back.

Ceilings are also unusual because they hold water. Drywall and insulation above a ceiling can fill like a sponge, and when the weight gets high enough the sheet sags or drops. A bulge that looks small can be holding gallons.

When a ceiling leak is an emergency

Treat a ceiling leak as urgent when it is dripping, when drywall is sagging or bubbling, when water is near a light fixture or when the leak came from a storm. Sagging means the drywall is carrying weight it was not built to carry.

Do not stand under a sagging ceiling

Keep people and pets out of the room. If water is running through a light fixture, do not touch the switch, and cut power to that circuit only if you can reach the panel dry. Call your electric provider or 911 for sparks or a burning smell before calling the restoration line.

What to do before the crew arrives

  • Move furniture and belongings away from the drip.
  • Put a bucket under the leak, and relieve a bulge by poking a small hole only if you can do so safely and the water can be caught.
  • Photograph the stain, the drip and the room before cleanup.
  • Turn off the water supply if a plumbing line above is the source.
  • Call the 24/7 line for same-day help when crews are available.

Finding the source above the ceiling

Crews use a moisture meter and thermal camera from below to trace the wet area, then open the ceiling where needed. In Dickinson the source is commonly a roof leak after a storm, a failed supply or drain line, or a clogged condensate line on an air conditioner in the attic.

Roof repair and plumbing repair belong to other trades, and we tell you what we find so you can call the right one.

The restoration side starts with controlling the water. Drywall that is wet but firm can sometimes be dried with equipment. Drywall that is soft, stained through or sagging is cut out. Wet insulation above it is removed as well, because insulation holds moisture against the framing and the next sheet.

Repairing the ceiling afterward

After drying, the opening is patched, taped, textured and painted. Textured ceilings take careful matching, and a patch that is done too soon, before the framing is dry, can show stains again. The drying readings are the checkpoint. For wet walls that sit beneath the same leak, the wall and drywall page covers the same decisions.

Storms and roof leaks around Dickinson

Gulf Coast storms push rain sideways under shingles and flashing. After a storm, a ceiling stain may appear days later when insulation finally saturates. The storm water damage page covers wind-driven rain and openings in the building envelope. If your roof needs repair, ask the roofer to document the leak location, because it helps with the claim.

Ceiling work follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, which calls for finding and measuring the wet area before any material is opened or removed.

Texas Department of Insurance guidance says sudden and accidental water damage is often covered and gradual leaks often are not. Your insurer decides coverage, so report the loss promptly and keep photos of the source.

Ceiling water damage questions

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Dickinson?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes ceiling leak calls at any hour, and crews are dispatched by address and loss size. Same-day service is the goal when crews are available. Keep people away from sagging areas while you wait.

Can a water-stained ceiling be repainted without repair?

Only if the source is fixed and the drywall is dry and firm. Painting over damp drywall traps moisture and the stain returns.

Should I poke a hole in a bulging ceiling?

A small hole can relieve trapped water, but only do it if you can stay clear and catch the water. If the ceiling is sagging heavily, stay out and call.

Will the repair match my ceiling texture?

Texture matching is part of the finish work. The patch is blended after drying so it does not stand out.
