When water is spreading in Webster

The city's own flood study says flooding in Webster mostly results from large rain events that overwhelm channels and drainage systems with runoff from upstream, and from storm surge off Galveston Bay during tropical weather. The primary stream is Clear Creek, a 197 square mile watershed that separates Harris County from Galveston and Brazoria counties.

Harvey in Webster

A city newsletter described August 27, 2017 as the day Harvey poured nearly 50 inches of rain on Webster. The same newsletter reported that the water and wastewater systems stayed operational and the drinking water stayed safe. That separates a flood loss from a water-supply loss. Even when the utility works, a house can take on floodwater, and the restoration is about the structure.

Commercial corridor and homes

Interstate 45 runs along Webster's west side with exits 23 to 25, which puts shops, restaurants and offices close to the highway. Our commercial crews handle those losses, and the commercial water damage page explains containment, downtime and records. Homes in the area need the same fast extraction, and the water extraction page covers it.

Permits and records

Webster's website lists building permits, inspection forms and FEMA flood panels. If your property is in a flood hazard area, ask the Building Division about elevation rules before you rebuild. Keep photographs of the waterline, a list of removed materials and the crew's moisture readings, since the city and your insurer may each ask for them.

Services Webster asks for

Creek and rain flooding lead, then commercial losses and plumbing failures. The flood damage cleanup page covers Category 3 water. The burst pipe page covers supply lines. The League City page covers the town that borders Webster to the south.

Clear Creek and the county line

Clear Creek marks the county line between Webster and League City, and I-45 bridges it. That matters on a bad day. Emergency alerts, road closures and drainage maintenance may come from different agencies on the two sides of the creek, and a property that sits close to the line can be confused about who to call.

If you are unsure, check the city name on your tax or utility bill, and give the dispatcher your street address so the crew can plan the route around closed roads.

Insurance

The Texas Department of Insurance says home policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and usually exclude floods, and federal flood policies require a claim within 60 days. If your property is in a mapped flood zone, a mortgage lender may have required flood coverage, so check your documents.

Webster jobs follow the same ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration as any other, with documentation that fits the Harris County permit and insurance questions above.
