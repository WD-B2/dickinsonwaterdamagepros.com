That makes this page the place to talk about what heavy rain does to a house over several days.

When water is spreading in Santa Fe

Rain that falls for days saturates the ground and fills ditches, and water then moves toward the lowest part of the lot, which may be the house. Look for water at door thresholds, under garage doors and along the base of exterior walls. Water that enters this way is Category 3 and calls for extraction, removal of wet porous materials and cleaning.

Stop the inflow

If water is coming from outside, sandbags or towels at doors slow it. If a pipe is the source, close the valve.

Mind the electricity

Do not touch outlets or appliances standing in water. Cut breakers only if the panel is dry.

Save what is high

Lift photos, documents and electronics, and park vehicles on higher ground.

Skip the floodwater

Keep children and pets out of water that came from ditches or the street.

Ditches, culverts and lot drainage

The City of Santa Fe lists culvert permit information on its website, and a blocked or undersized culvert can back water into a yard. After any water event, check the culvert in front of your property and tell the city if it is blocked. A restoration crew dries the house but cannot fix the drainage, and the city or a contractor can.

Permits, utilities and who to ask

Santa Fe has its own building and permitting office, so repairs inside the city limits are permitted by the city and not by Galveston County. Water service can come from the city or from a water district depending on the address, so ask which provider sends your bill before you call about a main.

We dispatch to both ZIP codes, 77510 and 77517, and the dispatcher can confirm a specific address.

Drying after days of rain

When it rains for days, the ground stays saturated and the air stays humid, so drying a house takes longer than it would in a dry spell. Crews close the affected rooms, run dehumidifiers continuously and check the framing and slab with moisture meters until the readings match the dry reference. Opening windows does not help, because outdoor air carries the same moisture.

Expect equipment to run for several days, and expect the crew to return for readings. The structural drying page explains the steps.

Insurance in a heavy-rain town

The Texas Department of Insurance says home policies do not cover flood damage from rising water, while some cover rain that enters through an opening made by wind or hail. Know which of those describes your loss before you call your agent. Photographs of the waterline, the entry point and every wet room help either claim.

Whatever the address in Santa Fe, drying is done to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, so readings and records look the same from one job to the next.
