Lennar describes Bayou Lakes as a master-planned community in Dickinson with a swimming pool, splash pad, playground and walking trails. The builder lists single-story and two-story floor plans, and several of the two-story plans put a game room and bedrooms on the second floor. That layout shapes the kind of water loss that is most common in homes like these.

Why upstairs plumbing matters

When a supply line, shower valve or air conditioner drain fails on the second floor, the water does not stay there. It runs through the floor, into the ceiling below and down wall cavities. A small upstairs leak can become a wet living room ceiling within hours.

Our ceiling water damage repair page explains the first-hour steps, including how to relieve a bulging ceiling safely. If the source is an air conditioner drain pan or clogged condensate line, see AC leak water damage.

If water is coming through a ceiling

Shut off the water

Close the main valve or the fixture valve, and open a low faucet to relieve pressure.

Turn off the air conditioner

If the leak is from the unit or its drain line, switch it off at the thermostat.

Keep clear of sagging ceilings

Do not stand under a bulging ceiling. Place a bucket and call.

Protect electrical

Do not use light fixtures or outlets in wet areas. Cut breakers only if the panel is dry.

Slab-on-grade homes and ground floor leaks

Many newer Texas homes are built on slabs, so a leak on the ground floor often sits under flooring or inside a wall. The signs are a warm spot on the floor, a high water bill or damp baseboards. The slab leak water damage page describes how the leak is located and the floor is dried.

For appliances, dishwashers, refrigerator lines and water heaters are common sources. See appliance leak water damage and water heater leak cleanup.

Drying and rebuilding

After extraction, the crew sets air movers and dehumidifiers and checks moisture readings on each visit. The structural drying page describes the process. Drying to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard aims to avoid tearing out sound materials and to stop mold from getting a start.

If a ceiling or wall must be opened, the crew tells you what is coming out and why before cutting.

Community rules, builder paperwork and insurance

Homes in a planned community usually belong to a homeowners association. Check your community documents before exterior repairs. If your home is recent, ask whether a builder warranty still applies to the failed part, because that can affect who pays for a repair of the source.

For the water damage itself, call your insurer promptly. Ask what your policy covers, since we do not promise coverage. We keep dated moisture logs, photographs and a written scope for the adjuster.

Bayou Lakes water damage questions

A leak upstairs is showing in my living room ceiling. What first?

Shut off the water at the main or fixture valve, stay clear of any sagging area and call. The crew will relieve trapped water and check how far it traveled.

Could my air conditioner be the source of a ceiling stain?

Yes. A clogged condensate line or a full drain pan can leak into the ceiling below. Switch the unit off at the thermostat and call so the crew can trace it.

Does a new home have a builder warranty that applies?

It might. Ask the builder whether a warranty covers the failed part. That is separate from the cleanup and from your insurance claim.

Do I need approval from my homeowners association?

Interior repairs usually do not, but exterior changes can. Review your community documents or ask the association.

Did Bayou Lakes ever flood?

We have not verified any flooding history for this community and do not claim one. Look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

Do you answer calls at night?

Yes. The emergency line is answered around the clock, including weekends and holidays.
