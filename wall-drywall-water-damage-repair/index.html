Wall and Drywall Water Damage in Dickinson, TX

Wet drywall can look fine and be soaked inside the cavity. Call to talk through wall and drywall water damage repair in Dickinson, TX.

Call (844) 950-6900See all services

Wall and drywall water damage repair in Dickinson, TX is a question of what is inside the wall. Drywall is gypsum between sheets of paper, and it wicks water upward from a wet floor and absorbs it from a leaking pipe behind it. The paper face can look intact while the core is soft, and the cavity behind it holds insulation and framing that may be wet as well.

Deciding whether to dry the wall or cut it out depends on the water category, the time and the readings. Clean water caught within a day or two often allows drying through small openings. Contaminated water or long exposure means removal.

Signs of wet drywall

  • Stains, bubbling paint or peeling wallpaper.
  • Soft or crumbly spots when pressed.
  • Baseboards that have swollen or separated.
  • A musty smell or visible mold.
  • Cracks and sags where the sheet is heavy with water.

What to do right away

  1. Stop the source of the water.
  2. Remove baseboards if you can do so without tearing the wall, so air can reach the bottom of the sheet.
  3. Move furniture away from wet walls.
  4. Photograph stains with a ruler or tape for scale.
  5. Call us for moisture mapping.

How crews decide

A moisture meter reads the drywall at several heights, and a thermal camera shows cool, damp areas through the paint. Wet drywall is cut with a flood cut, a straight line a few inches above the highest wet reading, so the repair can be made with a clean seam. Wet insulation is removed because it holds water against the studs.

When the damage is limited and the water was clean, crews may drill small holes or remove baseboards and use injection systems to blow dry air into the cavity. This saves the wall but takes careful monitoring. Readings must reach the dry standard before a repair begins.

Walls and the lower floor

Water from floors wicks up drywall by capillary action, often farther than the visible stain. That is why a small puddle can lead to an eight or twelve inch flood cut. The carpet water damage page and the ceiling water damage page describe the neighboring decisions for floors and ceilings.

Finishing the repair

After drying, new drywall is hung, taped and finished, then primed and painted. Painting a corner without blending the rest of the wall often leaves a visible difference, so full-wall painting is common. Texture matching adds to the work.

If the wall holds electrical wiring or plumbing, a licensed professional should inspect it before it is closed. The City of Dickinson Building Permits and Inspections office at 4403 Highway 3 can say when permits apply.

Mold and the cavity

Mold needs moisture and time. A wall cavity that stays wet is a likely place for growth, which is another reason to dry quickly and check the readings. Health questions about mold belong with a physician.

Plaster, paneling and wallpaper

Older homes may have plaster, wood paneling or wallpaper instead of plain drywall. Plaster dries slowly and can crumble if it stays wet. Paneling may warp and the cavity behind it holds moisture. Wallpaper needs to come off before the wall can dry because it traps water.

If your walls are not standard drywall, tell the crew when you call, because the drying plan and the repair differ for each material.

Home policies often cover sudden leaks that damage walls, and the Texas Department of Insurance advises photographing damage before cleanup.

Wall and drywall questions

Can wet drywall be dried instead of replaced?

Sometimes, when the water was clean and caught early and the sheet is firm. Soft or stained drywall is usually replaced.

How high should the cut go?

Usually a few inches above the highest wet reading. The meter decides.

Will paint hide a water stain?

Only after the cause is fixed and the wall is dry. Otherwise the stain returns.

Do I have to remove insulation?

Wet insulation is usually removed, since it keeps the cavity wet.
