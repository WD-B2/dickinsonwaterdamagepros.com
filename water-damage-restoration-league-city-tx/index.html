Water damage restoration in League City, TX has one feature that sets it apart from nearby towns: Clear Creek runs along the northern edge of the city, and the Lower Clear Creek and Dickinson Bayou watershed study treats the two waterways as one flood problem.

A League City home can be hit by creek water, by street drainage or by a failure inside the walls, and each calls for a different first move.

When water is spreading in League City

Floods lead the calls here, so this page starts with them. Community Impact reported that League City neighborhoods saw water rise about a foot during Hurricane Harvey, according to city drainage study documents, and The Weather Channel's list of NOAA Harvey reports includes 43.38 inches at the National Weather Service forecast office in League City.

A foot of floodwater is enough to ruin flooring, insulation and the bottom of every wall.

Call the line the moment water is moving into the house, and read the flood damage cleanup page for what the crew will do. Floodwater is Category 3, so porous materials come out instead of drying in place.

Shut the main valve

If a pipe is the source, close the main water valve first and open one low faucet to relieve pressure.

Power down wet areas

Cut breakers only if the panel is dry and reachable. If water is near outlets, stay out and call the electric provider or 911.

Lift what you can

Raise rugs, boxes and small furniture off the floor, and move electronics upstairs.

Keep people out

Children and pets stay out of floodwater and anything that smells like sewage.

Creek flooding, street drainage and older mains

League City has been working on the problem. The city has pursued drainage projects since Harvey, including a five million dollar study of the Lower Clear Creek and Dickinson Bayou watershed, and a second phase of the flood mitigation plan was approved in 2025. Those projects take years, and they do not change what happens inside a house tonight.

Some losses come from plumbing. The city has been replacing water mains and service lines in the Clear Creek Heights, Highland Terrace and Pecan Forest subdivisions, which are older neighborhoods where supply piping may also be aging. If you lose pressure or find water at the curb, report it to the city's utility line, and call us if it has reached the house.

Which services League City homeowners ask for

Floods and storm water lead, followed by plumbing failures. The burst pipe water damage page covers supply lines. The water extraction page covers pumping and the first hours. The structural drying page explains the drying that follows. For the nearby bayside community to the northeast, see our Bacliff page.

Utilities and permits in League City

League City provides water and sewer service itself and bills through its utility billing office at City Hall on West Walker Street. After the 2021 winter storm, the council approved a one-time water rate decrease, and the city's billing office can say whether any adjustment applies if a leak drove your bill up. For rebuilding permits, ask League City's building department, not Dickinson's, since permits follow the address.

Insurance works as it does elsewhere. The Texas Department of Insurance says home policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and usually exclude floods, and a federal flood policy requires a claim within 60 days. Photograph every wet room before cleanup.

Neighborhood notes

Bayou Brae and the Pecan Orchard area appear on League City's list of drainage projects. A Bay Ridge resident told Community Impact that the Harvey rain came down like a turned-on faucet. If you live near a ditch or a retention pond, check how fast it drains after rain, and tell the dispatcher when you call so the crew knows what to expect.

What to do before a crew arrives in League City

  • Shut off the water at the main valve if it is safe.
  • Stay clear of outlets and cords in wet rooms.
  • Move small valuables to dry surfaces.
  • Photograph the damage and keep damaged parts.

League City water damage questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in League City?

We cover ZIP codes 77573 and 77574, whether the property sits near Clear Creek, along the SH 3 corridor or close to I-45. The dispatcher confirms any address near the edge of a ZIP.

Is emergency water removal available in League City?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls at all hours, and crews are dispatched by address and loss size. Same-day service is the goal when crews are available. Stop the source and keep people out of contaminated water while you wait.

Who handles my water bill if a leak raised it?

The City of League City utility billing office handles League City accounts. Ask them whether a leak adjustment applies, and keep the plumber's invoice.

Does the creek flood policy matter for restoration?

It matters for your insurance, not for the cleanup itself. Standard home policies usually exclude floods, so ask your agent whether you hold flood coverage.
